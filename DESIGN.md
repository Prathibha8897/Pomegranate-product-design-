## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-fashion editorial luxury, botanical cellular science, and haute perfumery aesthetics. Designed for an ultra-discerning global clientele who demand uncompromising craftsmanship and sensory indulgence, the interface feels like stepping into a private salon illuminated by warm low-light amber and jewel tones.

The visual direction fuses High-Contrast Editorial Minimalism with Tactile Crystalline Glassmorphism. Deep, atmospheric crimson-tinted obsidian substrates set an intimate theatrical stage, allowing high-resolution product photography with specular lacquer highlights and champagne metallic details to gleam effortlessly. Generous negative space, whisper-fine filigree borders, and dramatic typographic contrast create an air of timeless quiet luxury.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from ripe jewel arils, high-fire ceramic glazes, and heirloom gold leaf:

- **Primary (`#8B0E1E`)**: Royal Pomegranate Crimson. Used for signature interactive moments, primary action focal points, and glowing state indicators.
- **Secondary (`#E0C38C`)**: Champagne Gold. Evokes fine jewelry detailing, delicate badges, key metrics, and typographic accents.
- **Tertiary (`#4A060F`)**: Nocturnal Burgundy. Serves as a richer tonal bridge for elevated cards, modal panels, and ambient backdrops.
- **Neutral (`#130A0B`)**: Obsidian Wine. A warm, near-black grounding base that eliminates harsh sterile blacks in favor of deep viticultural luxury.

For functional surfaces, employ translucent variations of obsidian (`rgba(19, 10, 11, 0.75)`) bordered by micro-fine gold foils (`rgba(224, 195, 140, 0.2)`). Text relies on Ivory Pearl (`#FDFBF7`) for display reading and Muted Alabaster (`#B8ABA8`) for secondary descriptive narrative.

## Typography

The typographical cadence balances the couture elegance of Playfair Display with the pristine, modern geometry of Plus Jakarta Sans. 

- **Editorial Headings**: Set in Playfair Display with unhurried tracking and stately proportions. Headlines celebrate the sensory and scientific qualities of the formulation.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy**: Set strictly in all-caps Plus Jakarta Sans with wide tracking (`0.15em` to `0.25em`). This mimics luxury product packaging stamps, ingredient annotations, and clinical certifications.
- **Editorial Body**: Rendered in light and regular weights of Plus Jakarta Sans to maintain high legibility against deeply saturated and dark backdrops.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy champions spatial unhurriedness. Luxury requires room to breathe; dense or cramped UI destroys the premium illusion.

- **Grid Architecture**: A disciplined 12-column layout on desktop scales to a 6-column grid on tablet and a 4-column structure on mobile. Column spans favor asymmetry (e.g., 5-column editorial descriptions offset against 7-column hero object presentations).
- **Rhythm & Bleed**: Full-bleed hero visuals with atmospheric vignettes lead into generous vertical section paddings (between `4rem` and `6rem`). 
- **Content Framing**: Critical commerce and skin diagnostic components exist within constrained luxury viewports (`max-width: 1320px`), ensuring visual tension remains centered and theatrical.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through liquid glassmorphism and radiant backlights rather than utilitarian drop shadows:

- **Atmospheric Glows**: Radial blooms of saturated pomegranate crimson (`rgba(139, 14, 30, 0.35)`) and warm amber (`rgba(224, 195, 140, 0.12)`) sit behind key focal points (bottles, hero CTAs, and featured formulations), simulating studio rim lighting.
- **Vitreous Panels**: Floating cards utilize multi-layered glass styling: a base of `rgba(28, 14, 17, 0.65)` with a 24px backdrop blur, reinforced by an inner bevel highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`) and an ultra-fine perimeter line of `rgba(224, 195, 140, 0.18)`.
- **Z-Index Hierarchy**: Modal dialogs and checkout drawers inherit a deep ruby-tinted darkroom scrim with progressive background blur (`blur(12px)`), focusing total attention on the transaction.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an architectural Soft silhouette (`roundedness: 1`). 

Corners are slightly tempered with subtle radius curves (`4px` for inputs and action buttons, `8px` for interactive panels and ingredient cards). This controlled curvature preserves the structured, high-fashion editorial profile of high-end cosmetic flacons and gold-rimmed ceramic jars while avoiding the sterile severity of pure 0px corners or the playfulness of heavy pill styling. Circular forms (`50%` radius) are reserved strictly for floating micro-actions, fragrance notes, and luxury shade-swatch selectors.

## Components

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary CTA**: Styled in solid Pomegranate Crimson (`#8B0E1E`) or framed Champagne Gold (`#E0C38C`), using all-caps tracking-wide typography. Embellished with a hair-thin `1px` border and subtle bottom-specular lighting. Hover states trigger a slow, golden sheen transition.
- **Secondary / Ghost CTA**: Obsidian glass base with a delicate champagne border (`rgba(224, 195, 140, 0.35)`). Text renders in crisp ivory.

### Cards & Vessels
- **Product & Ritual Cards**: Nocturnal glassmorphic surfaces edged with `1px` champagne filigree. Product imagery appears suspended in low gravity against subtle ambient crimson vignettes. Hovering gently lifts the card by 4px and increases the border luster.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Inputs**: Understated bottom-border-only styling or semi-translucent dark troughs (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`). Active focus replaces neutral borders with an illuminated champagne gold accent line accompanied by a soft, subtle glow. Floating placeholder labels translate into micro-caps.

### Chips, Pills & Badges
- **Formulation & Batch Chips**: Ultra-slim rectangular outlines featuring uppercase micro-labels (`label-caps`). Active selections fill with a translucent crimson wash (`rgba(139, 14, 30, 0.3)`).

### Selection Controls
- **Radio Buttons & Checkboxes**: Bespoke geometric selectors. Checkboxes feature fine champagne diamond or square checks; radios display a solid jewel-like crimson bead nestled within a gold ring upon selection.

### Bespoke Skincare Elements
- **Sensory Dosage Indicator**: A dedicated minimalist slider illustrating drop or gram titration, paired with tactile glass tick-marks.
- **Ingredient Provenance Accordion**: An ultra-clean list layout separated by hair-thin `0.5px` borders in muted bronze, expanding smoothly to reveal clinical efficacy metrics and harvest origins.
